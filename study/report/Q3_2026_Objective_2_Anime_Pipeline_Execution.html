3.2 Objective 2: Build an Agentic Anime Sketch Generation Pipeline

Phase 1: Foundation & Infrastructure

Frame-to-Stroke Foundation: Built a pipeline that converts one anime frame into ordered vector paths and a valid stroke-5 sequence with draw, pen-lift, and end states.

Clean Geometry Extraction: Added grayscale-aware centerline extraction, artifact removal, skeletonization, RDP simplification, and geometry-quality checks.

Stroke-Ordering Methods: Implemented directional, greedy nearest-neighbor, TSP, and continuity-greedy ordering for the same vector paths.

Initial Validation: Validated pen-state encoding, normalization, terminal tokens, and reconstructed geometry on representative anime frames.

Phase 2: System Completion & Training

Selected Ordering Method: Evaluation and documentation identified continuity-greedy as better than directional, greedy, and TSP; it became the centerline default.

Kinematics and Stroke Encoding: Applied Sigma-Lognormal timing before legacy stroke-5 conversion, while the centerline path preserves geometry without temporal resampling.

Sketchformer Dataset Packaging: Produced stroke-3 train, validation, and test archives with anime labels and Tok-Dict data aligned to Sketchformer’s discrete token IDs.

Dataset Scaling and Auditability: Added resumable, multiprocess preprocessing and audit manifests; 531 sketches were processed, with 516 accepted and 15 rejected.

Training Infrastructure: Integrated original TensorFlow Sketchformer and native PyTorch continuous/discrete training, checkpoint, and evaluation paths.

Phase 3: Scaling & Testing

Long-Sequence Expansion: Added a 512, 1024, 2048, and 4096-token curriculum; the planned 75, 150, 300, and 500–1000 comparison remains incomplete.

Preprocessing Stress Test: Accepted 9 of 12 hard samples with median geometry F1 at 2 pixels of 0.9914; three remained overlength and none failed processing.

Baseline and Anime Evaluation: QuickDraw memorization reached 0.996 token accuracy, while anime reached about 40%; neither result completes the planned benchmark.

Large-Scale Training Outcome: The 40,000-sketch run ended after a server restart, so no final large-scale checkpoint evaluation was available.

Go/No-Go Recommendation: GO for preprocessing and discrete R&D; CONDITIONAL GO for anime training; NO-GO until QuickDraw and mode-comparison targets are met.
